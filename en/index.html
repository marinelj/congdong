Pathway

Pathway

Student-athlete pathway planning

For basketball, across two school transitions: primary to junior high, and junior high to senior high.

  1. Stage 01

    Primary to junior high

    Aiming for a place at a traditional basketball school.

  2. Stage 02

    Junior high to senior high

    Make basketball a new path to school and to growing up

    We shape a training and admissions plan around each student's academics, basketball level and goals.

Junior high to senior high · Three directions

  • Path A

    Win a senior high school place

    For
    Students facing heavy pressure in the academic entrance exams
    How
    Specialist basketball training combined with admissions guidance, opening one more route into senior high

  • Path B

    Go for a traditional basketball school

    For
    Students who want a traditional basketball school but aren't yet at the level
    How
    Targeted work on the gaps, to prepare for the schools' selection trials

  • Path C

    Step up to athlete certification

    For
    Students already training with a traditional basketball school's team
    How
    Training and competition plans aimed at China's National Level 1 or Level 2 athlete rating

These are development goals, not a promise of school admission or certification results.

Every student is different. Ask Teacher He for a plan that fits.
